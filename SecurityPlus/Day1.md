Things I learned today:
- DIFFERENT SECURITY COTROLS: 
  1.TECHNICAL CONTROLS -
  -Controls implemented using systems.
  -Operating system controls.
  -Firewalls , antivirus
 
  2.MANAGERIAL CONTROLS
  -Administrative controls associated with security  design and implementation
  -Security policies, standard operating procedures 

  3.OPERATIONAL CONTROLS(using people)
  -Controls implemented by people instead of systems
  -Security guards, awareness programs

  4.PHYSICAL CONTROLS(Controls that limit someone's physical access to a room or device)
  -Limit physical access
  -Guard shack (checks everyone coming into particular area)
  -Fences, locks (to keep people out)
  -Badge readers (limit the access into certain areas within the building)

THE CIA TRIAD : sometimes referred to as the AIC triad 
-They are the fundamentals of security
1.CONFIDENTIALITY : Prevent disclosure of the information to unauthorized individuals or systems
2.INTEGRITY : Messages or information cannot be modified without detection
3.AVAILABILITY : Systems and networks must be up and running

*One of the way in order to provide this confidentiality is by encryption
 ENCRYPTION : Encode messages so only certain people can read it 
 - Access controls - Selectively restrict access to the resources
